# Sub-processors

Thapsus-HR is the processor and each customer is the controller. Personal data goes to a third party only if that party is listed here, with its purpose and location for each home region. Every entry is added **before** the integration is written. The public list per region is rendered from this file at `/trust/subprocessors` (generated data: `packages/core/src/trust/generated.json`, refreshed with `pnpm --filter @thapsus/core trust-data`; a unit test fails if it is stale). Customers are notified of changes at least 30 days before a new sub-processor starts processing their data: every change is added to the change log below, and administrators who subscribe in Privacy → Legal receive an in-app and email notice. The notice period (`subprocessorNoticeDays` in `packages/core/src/trust/legal.json`) is a draft pending legal review; see HUMAN_TODO.md.

## Customer-directed integrations

These are services the customer contracts with directly and connects to Thapsus-HR. They are the customer's processors (or the customer's own systems), not ours, and they are listed here for transparency. In particular the data-export destinations (the customer's own S3 bucket or SFTP server) are the **controller's own systems**, not Thapsus sub-processors: Thapsus-HR delivers data there on the customer's instruction, and what happens to it afterwards is the customer's responsibility. The delivery log records counts and timings only, never personal data. Nothing is sent to them until a customer admin **enables the integration** and, where the provider supports it, grants **per-customer admin consent**. Data flows only on the customer's instruction, from the tenant's home-region cell, over TLS. Credentials (OAuth tokens, client secrets, SFTP passwords and keys) are sealed with the cell's regional KMS key and are never logged. ### Destinations the controller configures (webhooks, audit streaming, API clients)

Webhook endpoints, SIEM streams (HTTPS endpoints and S3 buckets) and API clients are chosen and configured by the customer, who is the **controller**. They are not Thapsus sub-processors. Thapsus sends data only to destinations an admin of that workspace has configured, and stops as soon as the destination is disabled, deleted or revoked, or the feature is turned off.

- **Origin:** every outbound call starts in the workspace's **home region**: London for UK workspaces, Frankfurt for EU and Ohio for US. Thapsus never relays it through another region.
- **S3 audit streams:** these can only target a bucket in the home region. The residency-guarded AWS client factory refuses any other region, and this is tested for every region pair.
- **What is sent:** webhook payloads carry identifiers and the names of changed fields only. Audit streams omit the values of sensitive changes.
- **Safety:** destinations must be public `https://` addresses. Private, internal and cloud-metadata addresses are refused (SSRF protection).
- **Sandboxes:** a sandbox copies no destinations. It gets no webhooks, audit streams or API keys from production.
