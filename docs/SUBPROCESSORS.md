# Sub-processors

Thapsus-HR is the processor and each customer is the controller. Personal data goes to a third party only if that party is listed here, with its purpose and location for each home region. Every entry is added **before** the integration is written. Customers are notified of changes through the in-app sub-processor page (`/trust#subprocessors`) at least 30 days before a new sub-processor starts processing their data. That notice period is a draft pending legal review; see HUMAN_TODO.md.

## Customer-directed integrations

These are services the customer contracts with directly and connects to Thapsus-HR. They are the customer's processors (or the customer's own systems), not ours, and they are listed here for transparency. In particular the data-export destinations (the customer's own S3 bucket or SFTP server) are the **controller's own systems**, not Thapsus sub-processors: Thapsus-HR delivers data there on the customer's instruction, and what happens to it afterwards is the customer's responsibility. The delivery log records counts and timings only, never personal data. Nothing is sent to them until a customer admin **enables the integration** and, where the provider supports it, grants **per-customer admin consent**. Data flows only on the customer's instruction, from the tenant's home-region cell, over TLS. Credentials (OAuth tokens, client secrets, SFTP passwords and keys) are sealed with the cell's regional KMS key and are never logged. The outbound host allow-list in `apps/api/src/modules/integrations/http.ts` must match this table; a unit test fails if a host is added there without an entry here.
