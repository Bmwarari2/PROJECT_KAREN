# Thapsus-HR employee guide

This guide helps you use Thapsus-HR at work. It is written for phones, but everything also works on a computer.

Button and page names are in **bold**. They match what you see on screen in English (UK). If your company uses English (US), some names are different. For example, **Rota** is **Schedule**, **Clock in** is **Time clock**, **Payslips** is **Pay stubs** and **Annual leave** is **Vacation**.

Your company decides which features you can use. If you cannot see something in this guide, ask your HR team.

## 1. Put the app on your phone

Thapsus-HR is a web app. You do not need an app store.

1. Open the sign-in link from your invitation email in your phone's browser.
2. Open the browser menu.
3. Choose the option to install the app or add it to your home screen.
4. Open Thapsus-HR from the new icon.

If you lose signal, the app shows **You are offline**. Clock-ins you make still count. They are sent when you are back online.

## 2. Sign in

### The first time

HR sends you an email with a sign-in link. The link works once and lasts 3 days. If it has expired, ask HR for a new invitation.

The first time you sign in, your company may ask you to set up two-step verification: an authenticator app on your phone, or a passkey (your fingerprint, face or phone PIN). Follow the steps on the **Security** page.

### After that

1. Enter your **Workspace**. This is your company's short name, for example `larkfield`.
2. Enter your **Email** (your work email).
3. Choose one way to sign in:
   - **Sign in with a passkey**: use your fingerprint, face or phone PIN. This is the quickest and safest way.
   - **Email me a sign-in link**: we email you a link. It works once and lasts 10 minutes.
   - Your **Password**, then **Sign in**. If you set up an authenticator app, type the **Six-digit code** and choose **Verify**.
   - **Continue with single sign-on**: use this if your company signs you in with its own system.

Forgot your workspace name? Choose **Find my workspace** and enter your work email. We email you a list.

Forgot your password? Choose **Forgot your password?**, enter your workspace and email, and follow the link we email you. It works once and lasts 30 minutes. You then sign in with your new password (and your authenticator, if you use one). Your HR admin can also send you a reset link; they never see your password.

If you are not active for a while, you are signed out. Just sign in again.

## 3. Find your way around

On a phone, tap the menu button at the top left to see all pages. The main ones are:

- **Home**: your welcome page.
- **Me**: a summary of your time off, next shift, documents to sign, tasks, notifications and payslips, and **My details**.
- **Org chart**: find colleagues and teams.
- **Time off**: book holiday and see your balance.
- **Rota**: see your shifts.
- **Clock in**: start and end work.
- **Timesheets**: check and send your hours.
- **Tasks**: things you need to do.
- **Documents**: files and things to sign.
- **Payslips** and **Equipment**: your pay documents and the kit you were given.
- **Notifications** and **Security**: alerts and sign-in settings.

To sign out, tap the sign-out button at the top right.

## 4. Your details

Go to **Me** → **My details**. You see your record as HR holds it: your personal details, job, pay and contract.

To change your preferred name, pronouns, personal email or personal phone:

1. Tap **Update contact details**.
2. Type the new details. Leave a field empty to keep it.
3. Tap **Save**. The change applies from today.

To change your legal name, date of birth or nationality:

1. Tap **Request a change**.
2. Fill in only what should change, and the **Effective date**.
3. Add a **Reason (optional)** and tap **Send request**.

HR checks the request first. You can follow it under **My change requests**. It shows **Waiting for HR**, **Approved** or **Declined**.

For your address or emergency contact, ask HR.

The **Me** pages are new. If you do not see **Me** yet, go to **Org chart**, find your name with **Find a person**, tap it and use **Make a change** instead.

## 5. Before your first day

When HR invites you before you start, your **Home** page shows **Before your first day**.

1. Tap **Open your onboarding**.
2. Under **Documents to sign**, read and sign each document.
3. Fill in **Personal details** and **Emergency contact**, then tap **Save**.
4. Work through **Your checklist**. Tap **Mark as done** for each item.

The page shows how many days until you start and how much you have done.

## 6. Time off

### See your balance

Go to **Time off**. Each card shows one type of leave and how many days (or hours) you have available. Below that you see your **Entitlement**, **Public holidays**, **Taken**, **Booked** and **Carried over**. **Booked** includes requests that are still waiting.

### Ask for time off

1. Under **Request time off**, choose the **Type**, for example **Annual leave**.
2. Choose the **From** and **To** dates.
3. Add a **Note (optional)** if you want.
4. Tap **Send request**.

Your manager gets the request. You get a notification when they decide. Your request shows as **Waiting**, **Approved**, **Declined** or **Cancelled** under **My requests**.

### Cancel time off

Under **My requests**, tap **Cancel** next to the request. You can cancel a request that is still waiting. You can also cancel approved time off before it starts. Once it has started, ask HR.

### Who is away

**Who's away in the next 30 days** shows the time off you are allowed to see. Managers see their team. Public holidays are listed underneath. If a colleague is off sick or on family leave, it only says they are away.

### Add time off to your calendar

1. Tap **Add to my calendar**. A link is copied.
2. Paste it into your phone's calendar app as a calendar you subscribe to.

Your approved time off then shows as "out of office". Keep this link private. Anyone who has it can see the dates.

## 7. If you are ill

1. Tell your manager. Do it the way your company asks, for example by phone.
2. Your manager or HR records your sickness in Thapsus-HR.
3. If you are off for a long time, you may need a fit note. HR will tell you.
4. When you come back, your manager may have a short return-to-work chat with you.

Your health details are private. They are stored in a separate, locked part of the system. Only HR can read them, and every look is recorded. Your manager sees only the dates. Colleagues only see that you are away.

## 8. Your rota and shift swaps

### See your shifts

Go to **Rota** and open **My shifts**. **My shifts this week** lists your shifts. Use **Previous week** and **Next week** to look ahead. You get a notification when a new rota is published or a shift changes.

### Pick up an extra shift

Under **Open shifts**, tap **Request** next to a shift you want. Your manager decides.

### Swap a shift

1. Tap **Swap** next to your shift.
2. Under **Colleague**, type at least two letters of their name and choose them.
3. Tap **Offer**.
4. Your colleague taps **Accept** or **Decline**.
5. If they accept, your manager approves it.

You can follow everything under **Swaps and requests**. If a colleague offers you a shift, it shows there too.

## 9. Clock in and out

### Use the app

Go to **Clock in**. The page shows if you are clocked in, clocked out or on a break.

- Tap **Clock in** when you start.
- Tap **Start break** and **End break** for breaks.
- Tap **Clock out** when you finish.

**Last 24 hours** shows what you recorded.

### No signal?

You can still clock in and out. The app saves your times on your phone. It shows how many are waiting to sync. They are sent when you are back online, or tap **Sync now**. Times saved offline are normally accepted for up to 3 days. If a time cannot be saved, the app tells you. Ask your manager to correct it.

### Location checks

Some workplaces check that you are on site. This is off unless your company has turned it on for your country.

- If it is on, you see **Location check at this workplace** and a note from your company.
- Your location is read only at the moment you clock in or out, and only to check you are on site.
- Your exact location is never shown to anyone.
- If you are outside the site, your time is still saved. It is marked **Outside the site**.

### Use a shared kiosk

Some workplaces have a shared tablet, called a kiosk.

1. First, set your PIN in the app. Go to **Clock in** and find **Kiosk PIN**.
2. Type a **New PIN (4 to 8 digits)** and tap **Set PIN**. Do not use an easy PIN like 1111 or 1234.
3. At the kiosk, type your **Employee number** and your **PIN**.
4. Tap **Clock in**, **Start break**, **End break** or **Clock out**.

After too many wrong tries, your PIN is locked for a while. If the kiosk says it is offline, use the app or tell your manager.

## 10. Your timesheet

Go to **Timesheets** and open **My week**. For each day you see your **Rota** hours, your **Recorded** hours and the **Difference**. Big differences are marked. **Working-time checks** lists anything that needs attention, such as **You are still clocked in.**

When the week looks right, tap **Submit week**. Your manager then approves it or returns it.

| Status                   | What it means                   |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| **Not submitted**        | You have not sent it yet.       |
| **Waiting for approval** | Your manager needs to check it. |
| **Approved**             | It is done.                     |
| **Returned**             | Talk to your manager to fix it. |

## 11. Documents and signing

Go to **Documents**. Anything you need to sign is under **Waiting for your signature**.

1. Tap **Read** and read the document.
2. Tap **Sign**.
3. Type your full name.
4. Tick the box to agree to sign electronically.
5. Tap **Sign document**.

Thapsus-HR records your name, the time, your IP address, your device and a fingerprint of the exact document. This proves what you signed.

## 12. Payslips and equipment

### Payslips

Go to **Payslips**. Under **My payslips and tax documents**, tap **Download**. New ones are marked **New**. You also get a notification when one is ready. This can include year-end documents, such as a P60 in the UK. Only you and the payroll team can see your payslips. Your manager cannot.

### Equipment

If you are given a laptop, phone or other kit, it shows under **Equipment** → **Equipment issued to me**. Check the details and tap **Confirm receipt**. When you leave, HR will ask you to return it.

## 13. Tasks and notifications

**Tasks** lists things you need to do, with due dates. Tap **Done** when you finish one.

**Notifications** keeps all your alerts in an **Inbox**. Tap **Mark as read**, or **Mark all as read**.

Thapsus-HR can also email you or send alerts to your phone. Under **How you are notified**, choose which topics reach you by **Email** and which by **Device**, for example **Time off**, **Shifts and swaps** or **Payslips**.

To get alerts on this phone:

1. Under **Notifications on this device**, tap **Turn on**.
2. Allow notifications when your phone asks.
3. Tap **Send a test** to check it works.

## 14. Ask HR (UK only)

Your company may turn on Ask HR. If it is on, you see **Ask HR** in the menu.

- Ask HR is an AI assistant. You are not talking to a person.
- It answers only from your company's policy documents, such as the leave or sickness policy.
- It can make mistakes, so check the source it shows.
- It never makes or recommends decisions about you or anyone else, such as pay, promotion or dismissal.

To ask a question:

1. Type it under **Your question**. For example: "How much notice do I need to give for holiday?"
2. Do not include health details, other personal details or colleagues' names.
3. Tap **Ask**.
4. Read the answer and the **Sources**.
5. Under **Was this helpful?**, tap **Helpful** or **Not helpful**.

Your questions are not saved after you leave the page. For anything personal, or if Ask HR cannot help, talk to HR.

## 15. Your privacy rights

Your employer is responsible for your personal data. Thapsus-HR looks after it for them.

- Your data is kept in one region (UK, EU or US), chosen by your employer.
- You can ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted.
- To ask, contact HR by email, letter or in person. HR records your request. The law sets how long they have to answer.
- HR may check that the request really comes from you.
- Some records must be kept by law for a time, so not everything can be deleted straight away. HR will tell you what is kept and why.

You can read how your data is protected on the public **Security and trust** page.

## 16. Help and support

Your company may also have a **Help centre** and **Support** in the menu. These are new and may change.

- **Help centre**: search for help, for example "request time off". The question-mark button at the top of each page opens help for that page.
- **Support**: tap **New ticket**, fill in the **Subject** and **What happened?**, and tap **Send ticket**. Do not include health information. Thapsus-HR support can read your ticket, but nothing else about you unless your company gives permission.

## 17. Make it easier to use

- **Light or dark**: use the theme buttons at the top of the screen. Choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**.
- **Language**: choose **English (UK)** or **English (US)**. On a tablet or computer, the language list is at the top of the app. On a phone, it is at the bottom of the Thapsus-HR home page (the public website). Your choice is remembered on that device.
- **Less movement**: if your phone is set to reduce motion, Thapsus-HR uses less animation.
- **Screen readers and keyboards**: the app is built to the WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standard. It works with screen readers, keyboard-only use and large text. Use **Skip to main content** to jump past the menu.

## 18. Keep your account safe

Go to **Security**:

- **Passkeys** → **Add a passkey**: sign in with your fingerprint, face or phone PIN. Passkeys cannot be stolen by fake websites.
- **Authenticator app** → **Set up authenticator app**: get a new six-digit code every 30 seconds.

Good habits:

- Never share your password, PIN or sign-in links.
- Sign out on shared devices.
- If something looks wrong, tell HR straight away.
